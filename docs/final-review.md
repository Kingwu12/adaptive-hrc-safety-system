## What to review

All three authors should check that the methods describe what actually happened, that results and limitations are understandable, and that contribution descriptions are accurate. Flag a correction using a paper section/page or slide number and suggest replacement wording. Do not silently change values or infer controller assignments from block letters.

Suggested review focus: Zenan checks technical descriptions, analyses and figures; Luke checks coordination, deployment context and his talk segment; Michael checks apparatus/setup descriptions, geometry explanations and his talk segment. These are review assignments, not new claims about past experimental responsibilities.

The speaking plan assigns slides 1–7 to Zenan, 8–9 to Michael and 10–12 to Luke. Everyone needs to understand the research question, key results and limitations. Read the slide notes, practise the handovers, and do one timed team rehearsal below the strict ten-minute limit.
